Professional Rose Care & Cultivation

Expert Growing Guide

Growing commercial-quality rose blooms requires proper sunlight, moisture management, and precise pruning:

  • Sunlight: Ensure plants receive 6–8 hours of direct sunlight daily.
  • Watering: Water deeply at the base early in the morning, avoiding wet foliage.
  • Pruning: Prune deadwood regularly at a 45-degree angle just above an outward-facing eye bud.
  • Nutrients: Feed regularly with organic vermicompost and well-composted cow manure.
